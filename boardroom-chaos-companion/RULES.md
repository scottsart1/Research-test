# Monopoly: Boardroom Chaos — Four-Player Rules v1.4

Use the regular conventional Monopoly rules unless one of these house rules directly changes them.

## The practical core

1. **Exactly four players.** Each player controls one listed company and begins with the normal $1,500.
2. **Ordinary Monopoly stays quick.** Rent, bank purchases, auctions, mortgages, houses, hotels, taxes, Jail, and bankruptcy use the conventional rules unless stated below.
3. **The app ledger is official.** Log all completed payments, property changes, stock activity, filings, votes, taxes, mergers, rulings, and corrections.
4. **No real-world consideration.** Deals may use only in-game assets and promises.

## Whole-dollar money rule

All cash amounts are settled in **whole Monopoly dollars**. The app never creates cents.

- Player cash, bank cash, Free Parking, stock prices, stock-trade totals, rent, dividends, taxes, fees, loan principal, interest, and repayment balances are whole dollars.
- Percentage rates are shown as whole percentages.
- When an amount must be divided among several owners or shareholders, the app uses proportional allocation and distributes leftover dollars by largest remainder. The rounded pieces always add back to the exact rounded total.
- Property Tax remains rounded to the nearest $10 under the Tax Day rule.
- Share quantities and ownership percentages may still use decimals because they are not physical cash.

## Formal filings

A formal filing is a negotiated deal, stock offering, stock trade, corporate policy, standalone contract, or merger.

- Every formal deal proposal, policy, or contract costs a **nonrefundable $25 legal fee** when filed.
- The filing party or named sponsor pays the fee.
- The fee enters the Free Parking jackpot even if the filing is rejected.
- A merger also costs a separate **$200 merger fee**.
- Paying rent, buying from the bank, taxes, Free Parking, mortgages, and building are not formal filings.

A filing is enforceable only when the final recorded version has all required consent. Material changes cancel previous signatures.

## Approval randomizer

Every formal deal, stock offering, policy, and merger receives one electronic result:

- **10%:** Approved as written
- **40%:** Approved with one AI-defined condition
- **50%:** Rejected

There are no dice, rerolls, fairness scores, or invented reasons for the result.

For a conditional approval, DeepSeek may add one understandable condition, such as:

- One extra settlement round
- A 60% shareholder-vote threshold
- One temporary rent-relief obligation
- Public disclosure of the terms
- A temporary lock on a named asset

The AI may add only one condition and may not replace the entire bargain. Deal and merger parties must accept the condition. Policy shareholders vote on the conditioned policy.

## Two-round settlement

The settlement clock begins only after the final approval, final party consent, accepted conditions, and any required shareholder vote are complete.

The filing may execute **two full rounds later**. A condition may add one extra round.

Stock and property prices may change while a filing is pending, but the price stated in the filing stays locked.

## Company stock market

Each player-company starts with:

- Stock price: **$10**
- Founder-owned outstanding shares: **100**
- Maximum authorized shares: **150**
- One vote per outstanding share

After every completed four-player round, each active stock moves by a bounded semi-random percentage. The price never falls below $1. The movement has no reason, story, news event, or connection to company performance.

### Raising capital

A company may issue any remaining authorized shares at the market price locked when the offering is filed. The investor pays the company at settlement. The company may use the proceeds for properties, houses, hotels, taxes, or other expenses.

New issuance dilutes existing voting percentages. A primary offering requires a shareholder vote because it creates new shares.

### Trading existing shares

A shareholder may sell existing shares to another player at the market price locked when filed. It is a formal deal and therefore pays the legal fee, receives approval, and settles after the delay.

### Rent dividends

When a company receives rent:

- **80%** remains company cash.
- **20%** becomes a dividend pool distributed to all shareholders according to current outstanding share ownership.

The founder also receives the dividend attributable to founder-held shares. Stock ownership does not create direct ownership of individual property deeds.

## Dynamic bank lending

The bank has tracked cash and offers immediate loans to active independent player-companies.

- Opening quoted rate: **8%**.
- The liquidity base rate rises as the bank's cash falls and falls as cash returns. The liquidity component can add up to 18 percentage points.
- Once after every completed round, a random market spread from **-3 to +3 percentage points** is applied.
- The final quoted rate is rounded to a whole percent and cannot fall below **5%** or rise above **30%**.
- A company may have only one unpaid bank loan at a time.
- Bank loans are between **$50 and $500**, but the maximum may be lower when the bank has limited available cash.
- The bank keeps a $1,000 reserve and will not make a new loan from that reserve.
- A bank loan is immediate: it does not pay a legal fee, use the 10/40/50 approval system, or wait two rounds.
- The quoted rate locks when the loan is issued. Interest is flat simple interest, rounded to a whole dollar, and the full balance is due three rounds later.
- A loan becomes delinquent after its due round if it remains unpaid.

The bank must still honor required conventional payments such as mortgage proceeds even if its tracked cash is exhausted. Any shortfall becomes emergency bank liquidity. While emergency liquidity is outstanding, the rate is 30% and new bank lending is suspended. Later cash received by the bank repays emergency liquidity before rebuilding normal bank cash.

## Shareholder voting

Voting power is frozen using the ownership snapshot taken when a filing is created.

A normal vote passes only when YES votes exceed 50% of all outstanding record-date shares. Abstentions count against passage. An AI condition may require more than 60%.

Shareholder votes are required for:

- New share issuance
- Corporate policies
- Each side of a merger

A corporate policy may cover a development budget, financing plan, property strategy, dividend promise, or management restriction. It pays the legal fee, receives approval, receives a shareholder vote, and becomes effective after the settlement delay.

## Mergers

Full mergers are allowed.

- The acquirer pays the $25 legal fee and $200 merger fee into Free Parking.
- Both principal companies must consent.
- Each company’s shareholders vote separately.
- The stock exchange ratio is locked from the two market prices at filing.
- Pending deals involving either company must be resolved first.
- The merger settles only after the two-round delay.

At settlement:

- Target cash and property ownership combine with the acquirer.
- Target shares convert into acquirer shares at the locked ratio.
- The target stock is delisted.
- The target player no longer has a separate turn.
- The merged participants win or lose together through the surviving company.

## Property and development

Company shares do not place investors on property deeds. Properties remain owned by the player-company or by specifically logged direct co-owners.

All conventional rules still apply to:

- Complete color-group control
- Even house building and selling
- The physical supply of 32 houses and 12 hotels
- Mortgage restrictions
- Improved-property transfer restrictions
- Mortgage transfer charges

Capital raised through stock issuance simply increases the company’s cash available for those conventional investments.

## Tax Day

Tax Day occurs at the end of rounds 5, 10, 15, and every fifth round thereafter.

### Property Tax

Taxable property value is:

- Full printed price of unmortgaged property
- Half printed price of mortgaged property
- Original cost of houses and hotels

Property Tax is **3%**, rounded to the nearest $10, with a $10 minimum for any property owner.

### Net-Worth Income Tax

Net worth equals cash, property equity, outside-company stock investments, and enforceable receivables, minus every active or delinquent debt. A company’s own listed shares are excluded so its cash and property are not counted twice.

| Net worth | Tax |
|---|---:|
| Below $2,000 | $0 |
| $2,000–$2,999 | $50 |
| $3,000–$3,999 | $100 |
| $4,000–$4,999 | $175 |
| $5,000 or more | $250 |

Taxes enter the Free Parking jackpot.

## Free Parking jackpot

Taxes, legal fees, merger fees, designated fines, and qualifying bank charges enter the center pot. A player landing directly on Free Parking collects the entire jackpot.

## Antitrust

A company receives one antitrust review when it first:

- Completes a merger
- Controls all four railroads
- Controls three complete color groups

The electronic review randomly selects one result:

1. Cleared
2. Pay $200 into Free Parking
3. Collect half rent for one full round
4. No new houses or hotels for one full round
5. Auction one eligible unimproved property; if none exists, pay $150

The AI judge may interpret whether the trigger or property eligibility is satisfied, but it cannot invent a sixth penalty.

## AI Rule Judge

The AI judge may use only:

- These numbered rules
- Logged final contracts and policies
- Current game state
- Ledger evidence

A ruling must cite rule numbers, identify findings and uncertainty, give a concise remedy, and state confidence. It may not enforce a secret promise, invent a penalty, rewrite a signed agreement, or transfer assets when material facts are missing.

One appeal is allowed. The two uninvolved players vote. If they agree, their ruling replaces the AI decision. If they split, the AI ruling stands.

## Voice transcription and AI interpretation

OpenAI and DeepSeek perform different jobs:

- OpenAI converts recorded audio into text.
- DeepSeek receives the transcript and performs detailed categorization, contract analysis, approval-condition drafting, and arbitration.

A DeepSeek API status of `true` does not prove that microphone recording or OpenAI transcription is configured. A transcript must visibly appear, be interpreted, be reviewed, and be confirmed before changing the game. Voice commands are proposals only.

## Victory

There is no automatic 120-minute limit. The normal last-solvent-player rule applies, except that a completed merged company and its converted owners win or lose together through the surviving company.
